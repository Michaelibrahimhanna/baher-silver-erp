# Supplier Relationships Specification - Baher Silver ERP

This document details the supplier master data, raw bullion purchasing links, and accounts payable integration (`Account 2101`).

---

## 🤝 Supplier Entity Attributes

- **`supplierCode`**: Unique supplier identifier (`SUP-000001`).
- **`companyNameAr`**: Supplier business title (e.g. `شركة مصفاة الفضة النقية والسبائك`).
- **`contactPerson`**: Account manager / rep name.
- **`taxIdNumber`**: Official Egyptian Tax Card Number.
- **`accountCode`**: Linked Chart of Accounts Liability Code (`2101 - دائنون - حسابات الموردين`).
